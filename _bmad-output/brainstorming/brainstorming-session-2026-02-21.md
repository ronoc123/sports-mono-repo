## Final Features

### 1. Daily Check-In Streak
- Open app daily, earn vote rewards
- Consecutive day multipliers (Day 1 = 1 vote, Day 7 = 5, Day 30 = 20)
- Miss a day, streak resets
- **Purpose:** Daily retention driver

### 2. GM-Managed Daily Trivia
- GMs create daily trivia questions per org (team history, stats, roster, draft prospects)
- Correct answers earn votes
- Difficulty tiers for scaled rewards
- **Purpose:** Daily engagement + vote acquisition + fan education

### 3. Cross-Franchise News Feed / League Pulse
- Browsable feed showing GM moves, fan sentiment, franchise activity across all NFL orgs
- Fans can explore beyond their own team
- **Purpose:** Makes the app a daily destination for sports content

### 4. Trivia Leaderboards / "Franchise Scholar"
- Weekly and all-time leaderboards per org
- Visible badges and titles (e.g., "Franchise Scholar," "Org Historian")
- **Purpose:** Competitive status, long-term progression, power user retention

### 5. GM Polls
- GMs create custom polls in the org news feed
- Free participation (no vote currency required)
- Poll participation can earn small vote rewards
- **Purpose:** Expands GM content tools, fills feed, gives fans casual voice

### Daily Engagement Loop

Fan opens app (Check-In Streak) → Browses the news feed (League Pulse) → Sees a GM poll and votes (GM Polls) → Answers today's trivia (Daily Trivia) → Climbs the leaderboard (Franchise Scholar) → Comes back tomorrow to keep the streak alive
